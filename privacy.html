Privacy Policy

Effective 22 September 2026 · POPIA-aligned · Solutions Sinazo (Pty) Ltd

Solutions Sinazo (Pty) Ltd (Registration No. K2025/941001/07, "we", "us") respects your privacy and processes personal information in accordance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 ("POPIA"). This policy explains what we collect through Mzantsified, why, and your rights.

1. What we collect

CategoryExamples
Account identifiersName and email/profile identifier from your chosen sign-in method
Usage dataFeed preferences, which Plugs you view or save, device/browser type
Payment dataSubscription status and transaction reference from Ozow. We do not receive or store your card or bank details — these are handled directly by Ozow
Local device dataPreferences and session state stored in your browser's local storage, to keep the app working between visits

2. Why we process it

  • To operate your account and deliver your personalised Plug feed.
  • To process subscription payments via Ozow and maintain accurate billing records.
  • To improve the relevance of curated content.
  • To meet legal, tax and record-keeping obligations.

3. Sharing

We share the minimum necessary data with: Ozow (payment processing), infrastructure providers (Vercel for hosting, Cloudflare for DNS/security), and, where you opt in, WhatsApp for message-based updates. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Retention

We retain account and transaction data for as long as your account is active, and thereafter only as required by South African tax and financial record-keeping law.

5. Your rights under POPIA

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, object to certain processing, and lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) if you believe your rights have been infringed. Contact us using the details below to exercise these rights.

6. Cookies & local storage

Mzantsified uses browser local storage to remember your session and preferences on your device. We do not currently use third-party advertising cookies.

7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted transport (HTTPS) and restricted access to account data.
