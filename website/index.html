Questions, answered

Because you should not need a personal briefing.

Who is Mzantsified for?

It is for South Africans who are building businesses, hustles, income-generating ideas or entrepreneurial journeys and want easier access to useful opportunities, resources, money information and technology.

Is Mzantsified a marketplace?

Not in the traditional sense. Mzantsified is an access and discovery service. It brings relevant plugs into one daily experience rather than asking you to browse a marketplace catalogue all day.

Is it a job site?

No. Jobs and work-related opportunities may be relevant to some users, but Mzantsified is broader. Its MVP focuses on Business & Entrepreneurship and Money & Technology.

Is it a social network?

No. You are not joining another social feed to perform your life for the algorithm. Mzantsified is built around useful discovery and access.

What does “daily hook-up” mean?

It means a daily connection to useful things that may be relevant to your journey: opportunities, resources, information and technology. Your daily feed is the core experience.

Do I need to pay to try it?

No. The current trial is 7 days and does not request payment during the trial. Continued access after the trial is R100 for 30 days.

Will Mzantsified make me money?

Mzantsified does not guarantee income or business results. Its job is to improve access to relevant information and opportunities so you can make your own decisions about what is worth pursuing.
